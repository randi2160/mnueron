**Effective Date: May 18, 2026**

for AI tools, applications, and agents.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect

## 2. Local-Only Mode

Mnueron may offer a "Local Mode" where memories and chat history remain
entirely on your device.

In Local Mode:

- Data may never leave your device except for optional external AI model
  requests you initiate
- Mnueron may have no access to locally stored memories
- You are responsible for securing your local environment and backups

Because Local Mode is self-managed, Mnueron cannot recover lost local
data.
